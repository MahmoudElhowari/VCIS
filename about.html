Meet your consultant

Nadia Elhowari, RCIC

A licensed Calgary immigration consultant who has spent more than 12 years helping people build their future in Canada.

Her story

Dedicated. Experienced. Compassionate.

Nadia Elhowari is a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC, R514167) with over 12 years of experience helping clients across a wide range of Canadian immigration programs administered by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

Throughout her career, Nadia has guided individuals and families from many countries toward their immigration goals. She practises across most immigration streams, and her primary focus and most in-demand expertise is Group of Five (G5) refugee sponsorship and family sponsorship.

Nadia is known for her patience, professionalism and commitment to client care. She works with clients of all nationalities and gives personal guidance and steady follow-up at every stage.
