Frequently asked questions

What is an RCIC?

A Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC) is licensed by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC) and is authorized to advise and represent clients before Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) for a fee.

Nadia Elhowari's licence number is R514167. You can confirm it on the CICC public register (opens in a new tab).

Do you work with clients outside Calgary or outside Canada?

Yes. We are based in Calgary, Alberta, and work with clients of all nationalities across Canada and around the world. Consultations are available in person, by phone or by video.

Which languages do you offer services in?

Consultations and support are available in English and Arabic.

What is a Group of Five (G5) refugee sponsorship? Temporarily paused

A Group of Five is five or more Canadian citizens or permanent residents who agree to sponsor a refugee living outside Canada and to provide financial and settlement support, usually for 12 months. G5 sponsorship is one of Nadia's primary specialties.

Please note: new Group of Five applications are temporarily paused by IRCC. Contact us to discuss other options or to be ready when intake reopens.

How long will my application take?

Processing times depend on the program and are set by IRCC, and they change regularly. During your consultation we explain the current published processing times for your pathway and what you can do to avoid delays.

Can you guarantee my application will be approved?

No. All decisions are made by the Government of Canada, and no licensed consultant can ethically guarantee an outcome. What we do guarantee is honest advice about your eligibility and a carefully prepared, complete application.
